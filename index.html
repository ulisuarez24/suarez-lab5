NBA Draft Combine · Lab 5

Power forwards led in the full period; centers led in the recent period

Power forwards averaged 0.15 percentage points higher in 2000–2026; centers averaged 0.10 percentage points higher in 2015–2026.

2000–2026

Power forwards (n = 440) averaged 106.35%; centers (n = 217) averaged 106.20%. The mean difference was 0.15 percentage points, with power forwards higher.

The reported supplemental two-group Welch test did not find clear evidence of a group difference beyond chance (p = .526); this result was not independently verified. Reported Hedges’ g was 0.05, favoring power forwards and close to zero; it was not independently verified.

2015–2026

Power forwards (n = 159) averaged 106.30%; centers (n = 95) averaged 106.40%. The mean difference was −0.10 percentage points, with centers higher.

The reported supplemental two-group Welch test did not find clear evidence of a group difference beyond chance (p = .784); this result was not independently verified. Reported Hedges’ g was −0.04, favoring centers and close to zero; it was not independently verified.

The counts and means are reported from Lab5_Checks. The p-values and Hedges’ g are reported supplemental test results; neither the underlying workbook calculations nor these test results were independently verified.

Scouting takeaway

The observed mean gaps are small and reverse direction in the recent period. These results alone do not support setting different positional length benchmarks or changing prospect evaluations; no practical decision threshold was provided to judge whether effects of this size matter.
